# Contributing

[한국어](CONTRIBUTING.md) | **English**

Share reproducible layouts, failure conditions, and preflight improvements through issues or PRs. Include a self-authored dummy, Illustrator version, side structure, steps, and observations.

Do not submit client originals, contacts, font files, collected designs, or credentials. Distinguish physical proofs from screen comparisons and retain source preservation and explicit pairing contracts. You must have permission to share contributions under this package's MIT License.
