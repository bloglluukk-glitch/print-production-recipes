# 03 — Delivery preflight

[한국어](03_PREFLIGHT.md) | **English**

- [ ] Source, editable copy, and final PDF are distinct; rollback source is preserved.
- [ ] Variant and side counts match; front/back IDs, content, and orientation correspond.
- [ ] Dimensions and PDF TrimBox match; actual artwork reaches the bleed edge, not merely the BleedBox.
- [ ] Type, small lines, QR codes, logos, and effective image resolution remain suitable.
- [ ] ICC profiles, spot colors, overprint, transparency, fonts, and links meet printer requirements.
- [ ] Final PDF/X, compression, color conversion, and marks follow the printer's specification. Tool review PDFs do not replace this check.
- [ ] Check Page Information when unwanted filenames/dates appear at the top of a PDF; decide trim marks independently. [Adobe PDF options](https://helpx.adobe.com/illustrator/using/pdf-options.html)
- [ ] Reopen AI files and verify PDF counts, ledger, and every side rather than representative pages alone.
- [ ] Confirm long/short-edge flipping, imposition, folds, and finishing direction with the printer.
- [ ] Public sharing excludes client artwork, identities, contact information, quotes, workstation paths, PDF metadata, and hidden image information.

Bleed settings and physical bleed artwork are distinct. Use the printer's dimensions; the example's 3mm is not universal. [Adobe bleed background](https://helpx.adobe.com/illustrator/using/printers-marks-bleeds.html)

This is a working checklist. Digital comparisons do not establish physical proofing, binding, or color accuracy.

[Introduction](../README.en.md)

Tool PDFs are review canvases including actual outer bleed. The A5+3mm example has 154×216mm pages without a separate A5 trim-size TrimBox. Make final press PDFs separately from the AI using printer settings.
